Why insulate valves and flanges?

A bare valve can lose as much heat as several metres of bare pipe. Covers cut that loss, keep people safe from hot surfaces and protect fittings from frost.

  • Lower energy bills and emissions
  • Burn protection for staff
  • Frost and condensation protection
  • Stable process temperatures
  • Quick access for maintenance

FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot

How much does a cover cost?

Most standard valve and flange covers cost between £60 and £180 each, depending on size and temperature. Send us photos for a fixed price within two working days.

How long does it take?

After the survey, covers are usually made in 5 to 10 working days. We can fit them over a weekend so there is no shutdown.

Can I fit the covers myself?

Yes. Every cover comes with a fitting guide and quick-release straps. Most take under a minute to fit.

Will the covers survive outdoors?

Yes. We use a weatherproof outer for external covers, rated for UV, rain and frost.

Yes. We survey and install anywhere in the UK, or post covers to you.

How much heat does a bare valve really lose?

CNChris Nicholson
Managing Director

Most sites insulate their pipes but leave valves, flanges and strainers bare, because they need to get to them for maintenance. The trouble is that a valve has far more surface area than the pipe either side of it, so it loses heat fast.

The quick answer

As a rule of thumb, one bare valve loses about as much heat as several metres of bare pipe. On a steam line at 150°C running most of the year, that can add up to hundreds of pounds per valve, every year.

Want a figure for your site? Try our savings calculator.

What affects the loss

  • Temperature of the system
  • Valve size and type
  • Hours the system runs each year
  • Air movement around the valve

A removable cover cuts most of that loss, and comes off in seconds when someone needs to work on the valve.
